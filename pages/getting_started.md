## Getting Started with FCC Software and Analysis


* Join the [FCC physics software and computing Mattermost team](https://mattermost.web.cern.ch/signup_user_complete/?id=ea9j3u7pb3refrx4y57d8qhw9y&md=link&sbr=su)
* Find [mailing lists related to FCC physics software and computing activities]({{ '/mailing_lists' | relative_url }})
* Sign up for the [fcc-eos-access](https://e-groups.cern.ch/e-groups/Egroup.do?egroupId=10164506) e-group to get access to FCC samples on CERN /eos storage
* [Software tutorials](https://hep-fcc.github.io/fcc-tutorials/) covering first steps, event generation and fast simulation, analysis, full simulation, distributed computing, and software development
* The [software forum](https://fccsw-forum.web.cern.ch) is available for your questions and discussion topics
